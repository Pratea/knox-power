title=Knox Power
description=Power panels for Project Zomboid Build 42. Mount a panel on a wall, connect a generator and the WHOLE building gets power - every room and every floor, not just a 20-tile circle. Split the house into circuits with breakers, turn off what you don't need and save fuel.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Power panels:[/b] small, medium and large (4, 8 or 12 breakers). Craft them (Electrical 3) or find them in hardware stores, factories and, rarely, garages.
description=[*][b]Whole-building power:[/b] install a panel on any wall of a building (inside or outside), connect a generator within 12 tiles and the entire building is powered. Neighbouring houses do NOT get power.
description=[*][b]Breakers:[/b] open the panel to add breakers, name them ("Kitchen", "Upstairs"...) and switch them on and off. Put lights, TVs, radios, stoves, washers/dryers, fridges and freezers on a breaker with a screwdriver.
description=[*][b]Save fuel:[/b] devices on a breaker that is off can't be turned on and use no fuel. Fridges and freezers really turn off (they warm up and food spoils normally) and come back on with the breaker.
description=[*][b]Fuel consumption:[/b] the panel window shows fuel, consumption per hour and the estimated time left. Devices the generator wouldn't normally reach are charged from its tank.
description=[*][b]Main and secondary panels:[/b] one main panel per building/zone holds the generator; secondary panels (one per floor or wing) get power from it and have their own breakers. The main switch cuts everything.
description=[*][b]Zones for admins:[/b] on the world map, admins can draw zones (rectangles) so one panel powers a group of buildings - an office complex, a farm, a block.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer:[/b] the server decides everything and every player sees the same state.
description=[*][b]First person:[/b] the panel has a 3D model in first person with Viewpoint (optional).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How to use[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Get a power panel (craft or loot), a screwdriver and Electrical 3.
description=[*]Right-click the floor next to a wall of the building > Install power panel.
description=[*]Place a generator outside (within 12 tiles), right-click it > Connect to power panel (uses 1 electric wire), turn it on.
description=[*]Right-click the panel > Open panel to manage breakers. Right-click a device (light switch, fridge, TV...) to put it on a breaker.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Project Zomboid Build 42.21 or newer.
description=[*]Single player and multiplayer (install on server and clients).
description=[*]Optional: Viewpoint, for the 3D panel in first person.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Notes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]The generator itself still powers the usual 20 tiles around it, like any vanilla generator - keep it away from houses that shouldn't get power.
description=[*]Languages: English and Portuguese.
description=[/list]
